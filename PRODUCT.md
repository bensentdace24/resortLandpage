# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

React with Vite, selected by the user for an editable presentation mockup.

## Users

Prospective Samal Island visitors—especially couples, families, and small groups—comparing an accessible beachfront stay near the ferry wharf.

## Product Purpose

Present Samal Sands & Shores Beach Resort & Restaurant as an inviting island escape and make it easy to preview a stay and contact the resort directly.

## Positioning

A relaxed beach, pool, dining, and accommodation experience reportedly about five minutes from Samal Ferry Wharf.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page marketing mockup with an interactive booking estimate; it does not create real reservations.
- Resort information is based on public listings and must be confirmed directly before publication.
- Current public contact: 0968 721 3567 and samalsandshore@gmail.com.
- Check-in shown as 2:00 PM and check-out as 11:00 AM, following the supplied brief.

## Brand Commitments

Use the name “Samal Sands & Shores” and a warm, local, approachable travel voice. The user requested vibrant destination colors and aurora gradients, using the Travel & Tourism reference as inspiration.

## Evidence on Hand

Public sources report a white-sand beach, infinity pool, restaurant, villas/rooms, open cottages, water activities, pet-friendly access, and proximity to Samal Ferry Wharf. No verified owner-supplied photography, testimonials, availability, or current price sheet is available; presentation copy and estimates are labeled accordingly.

## Product Principles

- Make the island feel immediate and desirable.
- Put direct inquiry within one tap.
- Separate verified public details from illustrative presentation content.
- Keep every section and content item easy to edit.
